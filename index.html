About

I'm a high school student who fell in love with the space between hardware and software — the place where a single misplaced volatile keyword can crash a whole system, and where a well-placed DMA transfer can make a $3 microcontroller feel like magic.

Everything here is self-taught: no bootcamp, no formal curriculum — just datasheets, forum threads at 2 AM, and a lot of bricked boards along the way.

Next chapter: continuing my studies at PENS (Politeknik Elektronika Negeri Surabaya), to turn this self-taught foundation into formal, rigorous expertise.

02

The Accretion Journey

Like a planet forming from dust and debris colliding over millions of years, every board below accreted onto the skills of the last. Nothing here was built in isolation.

  1. 1

    U1 · THE GENESIS

    G-BOX

    ESP32 · OLED 128×64 SSD1306 (I2C)

    My first custom handheld console. Pure fundamentals: a real game loop from scratch, manual pixel rendering to a monochrome buffer, array-based memory management, and trigonometry (sin/cos) to make 2D physics feel right.

    Shipped: Flappy Bird · Space Invaders · Asteroids

  2. 2

    U2 · FIRST NETWORK

    ESP32-C3 Ultra Edition / AI-Pocket v1.0

    ESP32-C3 SuperMini · OLED 128×64

    First real dive into the internet and power efficiency. A "Battery Guardian" built on a physical voltage-divider circuit, a custom 6-button virtual keyboard wired to Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, and an internet-free radio system over ESP-NOW Mesh Networking.

  3. 3

    U3 · COLOR & DATA

    ESP32-S3 Media Hub v9.1

    ESP32-S3 N16R8 · TFT ST7789 (320×170)

    Leveling up to color displays and real web data. .mjpeg playback via DMA using LovyanGFX & JPEGDEC, live API integrations (ISS Tracker, NASA APOD, Groq AI), and BFS pathfinding for enemy AI in Pacman & Tank Battle.

  4. 4

    U4 · VISION & STABILITY

    SANZXCAM v6.1

    ESP32-S3 · 16-bit DVP Camera · MPU6050 · ILI9341

    A terminal-aesthetic camera. Kalman-filtered sensor fusion for electronic image stabilization, HDR triple capture, a sprite-restored Dynamic Island UI, on-device analysis via Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, and working steganography — payloads hidden in a JPEG's COM marker and a BMP's LSB bits.

  5. 5

    U5 · EDGE MACHINE LEARNING

    RYNE Engine — Bluetooth Music Player v1.4.0

    ESP32-WROVER · OLED SSD1306 · Bluetooth A2DP

    A Softmax multiclass "vibe classifier" that retrains itself every 5 minutes in PSRAM, a custom Bayesian recommendation model (RYNE-CRS) with power-law curiosity, and expressive OLED face animations synced to a dedicated FreeRTOS core watching the audio buffer.

  6. 6

    U6 · THE MASTERPIECE

    Accretion Phone — running nyx OS v84

    ESP32-S3 · ILI9341 resistive touch · MPU6050 · SD (SDIO 1-bit)

    The culmination of everything above: a custom smartphone built from scratch on bare Arduino framework, 13,000+ lines of code, zero real OS underneath. Glassmorphism lock screen, momentum-scrolling home screen with zero-flicker double buffering in PSRAM, 26 built-in apps, Gemini voice dictation via a hand-written JSON parser, OTA updates, and a Game Mode running Inferno — a 3D raycasting FPS holding a stable 16 FPS with persistent autosave.
